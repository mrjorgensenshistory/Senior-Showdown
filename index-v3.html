THE SITUATION

Every class needs to start saving now toward covering their Senior Package — the bundle that covers your entire senior year experience:

  • Prom — venue, dinner, DJ, photographer, decorations
  • Disneyland Grad Nite — $180–$259 per person + bus
  • Yearbook — $45 each
  • Senior Luncheon — your last meal together before graduation

That's thousands of dollars your class has to raise over four years. The less you save… the more you have to fundraise prior to October, or the cheaper everything has to be.

Start saving now = an easier senior year with more options. Just like life.

In this game, you'll learn:

  • 9th grade — How the forms and money procedures work
  • 10th grade — Haunted House, product fundraisers, building the bank
  • 11th grade — Bigger fundraisers, growing your savings for senior year
  • 12th grade — Prom, Grad Nite, pricing, budgeting — the real thing

Reality check: Classes get smaller every year as students transfer out. You might start 9th grade with 80+ students fundraising, but by senior year you could be down to 60 or fewer — and those are the ones who need to cover prom. Fewer students = fewer ticket buyers = harder math.

Every dollar raised in 9th–11th grade carries forward. Every dollar wasted is a dollar your senior class doesn't have.

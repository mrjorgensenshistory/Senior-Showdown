CODES — FORMAT & VERIFICATION
Each code has THREE parts: GRADE-IDENTITY-SCORE. Example: WAVE-S6KG-080
  WAVE / HAUN / HOCO / PROM  — grade tag (9 / 10 / 11 / 12)
  XXXX — 4-char hash of student’s name (use master list to verify)
  PPP — quiz score 000–100 (self-graded by the game)

To verify: Use code-master-list.html. Paste roster, get expected codes. Compare the first 9 chars (e.g., WAVE-S6KG). The last 3 chars are the student’s end-of-grade quiz score — auto-graded. 5 questions per grade, each worth 20%.

PART 3 — REFLECTION
Must reference SPECIFICS from their playthrough. Generic answers = AI / copied.
  1. Most profitable fundraiser: Personal. Common: HOCO ($3K), Spring Auction, Sadie’s at $20 + food, Haunted House.
  2. Regretted fundraiser: Should name a specific fundraiser. Game-over paths (skipped paperwork, skipped HOCO) = automatic 0. Or weak yields.
  3. Almost-pick venue + why changed: Should reference at least TWO venues by name (their pick + their alternate). Reasoning often: budget, vibes, bus cost.
  4. Hardest decision: Look for specifics: venue choice, ticket pricing, Pro vs Student DJ, whether to include food, skip a fundraiser.
  5. Surprising moment: Should name a specific fundraiser and a specific dollar amount or outcome. Generic = no credit.
🔍 HOW TO SPOT CHEATING
  1. Codes don’t start with right prefix (WAVE/HAUN/HOCO/PROM) ⇒ fake.
  2. 4-char identity suffix doesn’t match master list for their name ⇒ they used a different name in the game, or made it up.
  3. Wrong character names (Terry/Bobby) ⇒ can’t Google these — they didn’t play.
  4. Wrong specific numbers ($1,750 bus, $45 yearbook, 36 cars, +50% food, $200 Pro DJ) ⇒ guessed or copied from a friend.
  5. Part 2 ending balances are identical to another student’s ⇒ they shared a save or copied numbers.
  6. Reflection answers have NO game-specific terms (no fundraiser names, no dollar amounts, no venue names) ⇒ AI / generic / copied.
  7. Only some codes filled in ⇒ only played some grades. Partial credit at your discretion.
